How your Reading Compass works

The private 104-book target sets your yearly trajectory. Finished eligible books count toward both goals; partially read eligible books are shown separately as temporary pace equivalents, never as completed Goodreads books. A one-book grace zone keeps small day-to-day changes from becoming stressful.

A rough year-end estimate is available only after 28 days of 2027 and eight finished eligible books. No daily pages, reading sessions or exact habits are inferred from a percent or completion date.

Privacy and safety

Checking offline availability…

The library uses its existing isolated storage and backup format, separate from Disc Ledger. Saved custom-book details and actual reading records are kept on-device. No automatic cloud sync.

Online cover lookups contact Open Library and Google Books and show third-party image requests; private reading progress stays on your device unless you choose otherwise.

Protect your reading library

Device-only

Your saved preferences, custom books and reading records belong to this app. Export an entire SHA-256-checksummed library backup to keep a copy. Imports are checked before replacing anything.

An imported backup will replace the local library records only after an explicit confirmation. Disc Ledger backups are refused. Backups are readable JSON, not encrypted, so keep them private. On-device saving is not cloud synchronization; browser data clearing can still erase local records.
